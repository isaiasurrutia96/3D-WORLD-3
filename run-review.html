Reference and limits

Reference: SM64 clip 0x72, 72 frames, plus straight-running torso lean and playback. Preview speed is 32 game units per tick: 240 source frames/second, 0.30 seconds/cycle at 30 game ticks/second.

The rebuild preserves full 3D source rotations and anatomical segment directions, with a fixed conversion from native X-aligned bones into the approved body. No procedural swing, joint-angle clamps, foot planting or invented stride curves are used. The avatar's proportions, continuous skinning and shoe dimensions differ from Mario's, so silhouettes and exact ground contact cannot match simultaneously. A single fixed vertical offset avoids floor penetration over the sampled cycle; it does not correct individual contacts. The reference is reconstructed joint motion, not a recording or Mario's rendered mesh. This review covers steady straight running on flat ground, not acceleration or turning. The previous run is phase-aligned to the reference, rather than played at its independent travel cadence. Subframe interpolation is for inspection; the original game renders at 30 Hz. The native shoe vertices can also cross the nominal floor plane in this reconstruction.
